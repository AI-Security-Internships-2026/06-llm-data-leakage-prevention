## Issue #4 — Realistic Scenario Finding (Negative Result)

**Finding:** The KV-cache timing side-channel requires the private region to span
a large number of tokens (~2000+, i.e. ~128-192 KV blocks at block_size=16) to
produce a measurable prefill-latency gap. Short, realistic-length clinical/
financial/enterprise note templates (tens of tokens) do **not** expose a
detectable cache-hit/cache-miss signal under this attack.

**Evidence:** Direct probe test on live vLLM/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B:

| Probe condition | TTFT (ms) |
|---|---|
| Exact match (full cache hit) | 83 |
| Right name, wrong secret | 82 |
| Wrong name, right secret | 84 |
| Full miss | 84 |

All four conditions are statistically indistinguishable (~1-2ms spread), versus
a 489ms gap (87ms hit vs 576ms miss) in the controlled/padded two-stage template
from Issues #1-3.

**Conclusion:** Exact-value reconstruction via this timing side-channel is
demonstrated only on artificially large private-context templates. It does
**not** currently generalize to naturally-sized realistic prompts. This
directly satisfies Issue #4's requirement to separate bounded-task claims from
open-vocabulary/realistic-scenario claims, and to retain negative results
rather than tune the experiment to force a positive outcome.

**Tag:** `empirical` (measured on live vLLM server, 2026-09-14)

**Follow-up (not yet run):** Padding realistic templates to ~1500-2000 tokens
to determine the minimum private-context length at which the attack becomes
viable. Tracked as future work.

## Issue #6 — Cross-Backend Validation (SGLang) Finding

**Finding:** The KV-cache timing side-channel is confirmed on a second real
backend (SGLang 0.5.19). The two-stage exact-value reconstruction algorithm
does **not** transfer to SGLang without backend-specific block-alignment retuning.

**Evidence (corrected vLLM measurement, 2026-09-19):**

| Backend | Version | AUC | Timing gap | Exact recovery (ASR) | Median queries |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| vLLM (corrected) | 0.27.1 | 0.9999 | 1118 ms | **100% (10/10)** | 82 |
| SGLang | 0.5.19 | 0.9999 | 948 ms | **0% (0/10)** | 28 |
| Mock (reference) | — | 1.0000 | 1229 ms | 100% (10/10) | 53 |

**Conclusion:** Cache-timing oracle generalizes across backends (AUC 0.9999 on
both). Exact-value reconstruction requires backend-specific retuning for
SGLang's RadixAttention. Backend and algorithm effects are not conflated —
per Issue #6 acceptance criteria.

**Tag:** `empirical` (corrected measurement, live vLLM 0.27.1 + SGLang 0.5.19, 2026-09-19)
